Privacy Policy

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. This page does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, address or date of birth. There is no newsletter, no contact form and no user account. If you choose to type a blood sugar reading, height, weight, age or exercise answer into the calculator, that number is processed by JavaScript inside your browser and is not transmitted anywhere. It disappears when you close or reload the page. We do not keep a history of your results.

Medical Disclaimer

This site is for educational purposes only. It is not medical advice, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Every result on this page is an estimate calculated from the numbers you type in, using general published screening ranges that are the same for most adults. Your own doctor may deliberately aim for different numbers, because a target that is right for one person can be wrong for another.

Blood sugar ranges here are screening ranges, not a diagnosis. Diabetes and prediabetes are diagnosed by a doctor using a laboratory A1C test, a fasting plasma glucose test or an oral glucose tolerance test — usually confirmed on a second occasion. A BMI number cannot tell muscle from fat, and it is a rough guide at best after age 65. Water and protein suggestions are general starting points, not a prescription.

Always talk to your doctor, pharmacist or another qualified health professional about your own readings, medicines and targets — especially before you change what you eat, drink or take, and especially if you have kidney disease, heart failure, diabetes or take a diuretic or blood-thinning medicine.

Get help right away if you have chest pain, trouble breathing, confusion, fainting, or a blood sugar reading under 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L) that does not come back up after eating or drinking something sweet. Call your local emergency number.
