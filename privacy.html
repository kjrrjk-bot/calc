Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

The short version: this site has no sign-in, no accounts and no contact forms. The amounts, days and currencies you type into the calculators stay in your own browser and are never sent to us or stored on a server. The only outside services involved are Google, which shows the ads that keep this page free, and the map and travel links you may choose to open.

What this site collects

Calculator input

Nothing that identifies you. This site does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, address or passport details. There is no newsletter, no booking form and no user account. When you enter a currency amount, trip length, number of travellers, a shopping total or a number of transit rides, the figure is processed by JavaScript inside your browser and is not transmitted anywhere. It disappears when you close or reload the page, and we keep no history of your results.

Cookies and advertising

This site is supported by advertising delivered by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies or read device identifiers when you visit this page, and may use the fact that you visited it, together with your earlier visits to other sites, to choose which ads you see. Those are advertising cookies, not something we set ourselves; we do not receive your name or contact details from them.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can review and change the advertising topics Google associates with you, and switch personalised advertising off, in Google My Ad Center.
  • You can also opt out of personalised advertising from many third-party vendors at optout.aboutads.info, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner sites page.
  • If you switch personalised ads off you will still see ads; they simply will not be selected from your browsing history.

Your browser also lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking advertising cookies does not stop the calculators from working.

Server logs

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records ordinary technical information when a page is requested, such as the IP address, the browser type and the time of the request, in order to deliver the page and protect the service. We do not combine those logs with anything else and do not use them to identify individual visitors.

Links to other sites

Some links lead to other websites: our sister site Travel Korea Picks, Google My Ad Center, and the advertising industry opt-out page. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and once you follow a link this policy no longer applies. We do not pass any of your calculator input to them.

Children

This site is a general travel planning tool. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. For the advertising side, use the My Ad Center and opt-out links above; those controls belong to Google and its partners, not to this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Please check back if you want to see the current version.

About the estimates on this site

The calculators are planning tools, not quotes and not financial or legal advice. Exchange rates are mid-market rates published on the date shown and differ from what your bank or card issuer gives you. Budget figures are typical prices for the travel style you choose. Tax refund amounts follow one refund operator's published table, and the slip you receive at the shop or the airport counter is the final word. Transit fares and pass prices are the ones published by the Seoul Metropolitan Government and the pass operator at the time of the last check.

Prices, fares, refund rules and visa or customs allowances change without notice, and the rules that apply to you depend on your nationality, residence and how long you stay. Confirm anything you are about to rely on with the shop, the transport operator, the airport or the relevant government office.
